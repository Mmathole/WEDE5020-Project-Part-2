Our Programs

We run various initiatives focused on reducing child hunger and supporting education.

School Feeding

Providing nutritious meals each school day so children can learn, focus, and thrive.

Community Outreach

Supporting families and schools with food security programs and local community partnerships.

Advocacy & Awareness

Helping communities understand the link between nutrition, health, and education.

Ways To Help

There are several ways you can contribute to our cause:

  • Raise funds through your school, workplace, community, or personal fundraising activity.
  • Volunteer your time and skills.
  • Spread awareness about child hunger and malnutrition.

Brand and Partner Opportunities

Businesses and organizations can explore opportunities to support The Lunchbox Fund's nutrition programs and wider work. Please contact us for more information.

Volunteer and Raise Awareness

Support the cause by sharing information, encouraging others to volunteer, helping with fundraising activities, and spreading awareness about child hunger and education.
