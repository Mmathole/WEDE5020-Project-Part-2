Our story, our purpose

About The Lunchbox Fund

Learn about the organization, its history, mission and the communities it serves.

A meal can make room for learning

The Lunchbox Fund works to reduce the effects of hunger and food insecurity among vulnerable schoolchildren.

By supporting access to nutritious meals, the organization aims to help children attend school, concentrate on their education and improve their future opportunities.

Growing with communities

Our History

Discover key moments in The Lunchbox Fund's journey.

2005 A small beginning in Soweto

The Lunchbox Fund was founded in 2005 by South African model Topaz Page-Green. It began by feeding 100 children at a high school in Soweto.

Today Supporting children across the region

The organization has grown its work to support vulnerable schoolchildren in South Africa, Mozambique and Lesotho.

What guides our work

Vision, Mission & Values

Choose a topic to learn more about the purpose behind our work.

A future where every child can learn

Our vision is to foster education through nutrition by providing healthy meals to orphaned, vulnerable and food-insecure schoolchildren in townships and rural areas.

Upholding the right to education

Our mission is to help ensure that hunger and food insecurity do not prevent children from learning.

Working alongside communities

Our work is grounded in care, dignity and collaboration with the communities, schools and local partners who support children every day.

Our Team

The Lunchbox Fund is supported by board members, advisors, employees, fieldworkers and Food Mamas who contribute to its work in different countries.
