About The Lunchbox Fund

Learn more about the organization, its history, mission and people.

Our Story

The Lunchbox Fund works to reduce the effects of hunger and food insecurity among vulnerable schoolchildren.

By supporting access to nutritious meals, the organization aims to help children attend school, concentrate on their education and improve their future opportunities.

Our History

The Lunchbox Fund was founded in 2005 by South African model Topaz Page-Green. The organization started by feeding 100 children at a high school in Soweto. Since then, the organization has grown to provide millions of daily meals to vulnerable schoolchildren across South Africa, Mozambique and Lesotho.

Vision and Mission

The vision of the Lunchbox Fund is to foster education through nutrition by providing healthy meals daily to orphaned, vulnerable, and food-insecure schoolchildren in townships and rural areas.

Its mission is to uphold children's right to education by helping ensure that hunger and food insecurity do not prevent them from learning.

Our Team

The Lunchbox fund is led by a dedicated team of professionals who are passionate about making a difference in the lives of children. Our team consists of board members, advisors, employees, fieldworkers and Food Mamas who contribute to the organization's work in different countries.
