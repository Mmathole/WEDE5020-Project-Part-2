About Us

The Lunchbox Fund is dedicated to providing nutritious meals to children in underprivileged communities. Our goal is to improve the health and well-being of children, enabling them to focus on their education and future.

About The Lunchbox Fund


                The Lunchbox Fund works to reduce the effects of hunger and
                food insecurity among vulnerable schoolchildren.
                By providing nutritious meals, the organisation helps children
                attend school, concentrate on their education and have better
                opportunities for their future.

Our History

                The Lunchbox Fund was founded in 2005 by South African model
                Topaz Page-Green.
                The organisation started by feeding 100 children at a high
                school in Soweto. Since then, the organisation has grown to provide millions of
                daily meals to vulnerable schoolchildren across South Africa,
                Mozambique and Lesotho.
            
                
                 >

Vision and Mission

                The vision of the Lunchbox Fund is to foster education through nutrition 
                by providing healthy meals daily to orphaned, vulnerable, and food-insecure 
                schoolchildren in townships and rural areas. Its mission is to uphold children's
                right to education by ensuring that hunger and food insecurity do not prevent them from learning.

Our Team

                The Lunchbox fund is led by a dedicated team of professionals who are passionate about making a difference in the lives of children. 
                Our team consists of board members, advisors, employees, fieldworkers and Food Mamas who contribute to the organisation's
                work in different countries.
